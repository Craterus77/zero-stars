Zero Stars

A complaints-only public register for people who were not heard. No five-star side, no glowing reviews — a documented record of where a company, tradesperson, or service provider let someone down, from a faulty kettle to a house build gone wrong.

How it works

  • Anyone can read and search — free, no account.
  • Filing a complaint needs a free account, so you can track responses.
  • Every business gets a public right of reply.
  • Ratings run zero to −5 — never positive.

Fair use

  • Complaints must be truthful and first-hand.
  • Businesses may respond or flag a case as resolved.
  • Disputed entries go to moderation review.

Prototype / concept demo. All businesses, complaints and responses shown are fictional and generated for demonstration only — any resemblance to a real company is coincidental. Not a live service; nothing submitted here is published or stored beyond your own browser.
